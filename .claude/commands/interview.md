Read `skills/ai-interview/SKILL.md`, `skills/ai-interview/references/voice-first-studio.md` and `skills/ai-interview/references/career-workflow.md`. Start the local studio only for an explicit candidate request, using `python3 skills/ai-interview/scripts/career.py interview --doctor` before offering an authenticated local UI. Spoken questions and microphone answers are the default except Coding. Only confirmed transcripts are graded. Preserve strong baselines, fair rebuttal, and reference solutions after review. Do not mistake a stale study timestamp for forgotten knowledge; do not claim network-fetched 'latest' without a dated source.
